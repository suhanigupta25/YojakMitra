This website is to help citizens of India to easily navigate the schemes available with their proper description, requirement and criterias. 

The scheme cards have all related information which the applicant would require with an AI-assisted comparison tool where they can easily compare the schemes of their choices with a single-click. 

The YojakMitra platform also offers YojakMitraAI which is a scheme consultant where users can talk freely and ask there doubts related to the schemes.
